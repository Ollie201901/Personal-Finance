To Start Up
1)  Satisfy requirements as per requirements.txt
2)  Run app.py
3)  Open a browser and type in the following url: http://127.0.0.1:2000/

If you crash it...
1) Stop execution of app.py
2) Re-run app.py
3) Refresh browser

Note: the database is disconnected from the app due to it being unfinished and not stable.
